Tip 2

Read the claims, not just the abstract

An invented example. Flip the switch to see what actually counts.

ABSTRACT

A coating that improves how a sensor responds to chemicals in the air, with many possible uses.

CLAIM 1: WHAT IS ACTUALLY PROTECTED

A sensor comprising a silicon layer having a gap of less than 20 nanometres, and a coating applied within said gap, wherein the coating binds a target molecule.

The abstract is broad and friendly. The claim is narrow and exact.
